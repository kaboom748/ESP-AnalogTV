How color and H/V sync pulses work

YUV 4:2:0 color: Full-resolution Y (luminance), followed by U and V at half horizontal and vertical resolution. These are analog PCM8 amplitudes, not JPEG data. The 256 × 192 Color Stable mode sends every Y, U and V sample twice for smoother analog reconstruction (about 0.69 FPS). More samples per frame means fewer FPS. Signal defects appear as color shifts or noise.

H-SYNC: A 24-sample pulse starts EVERY line, with progressive lock and a narrow search window to reject false pulses. V-SYNC: A 168-sample signature starts EVERY frame: 48 low / 12 high / 48 low / 12 high / 48 low. Total frame length is unchanged.

Every line starts with a 24-sample H pulse. Each frame starts with a distinctive 168-sample V marker using reserved extreme levels. 640×480 mono: 1 spp ≈ 0.353 FPS (2.834 s/frame); 2 spp ≈ 0.185 FPS (5.394 s/frame). Both retain V12 radio processing at 120.000 PCM8 samples/s, with validated MPX4 transport when supported and legacy MPX3 compatibility.
