How color and H/V sync pulses work

YUV 4:2:0 color: Full-resolution Y (luminance), followed by U and V at half horizontal and vertical resolution. These are analog PCM8 amplitudes, not JPEG data. More samples per frame means fewer FPS. Signal defects appear as color shifts or noise.

H-SYNC: A 24-sample pulse starts EVERY line, with progressive lock and a narrow search window to reject false pulses. V-SYNC: A 168-sample signature starts EVERY frame: 48 low / 12 high / 48 low / 12 high / 48 low. Total frame length is unchanged.

Every line starts with a 24-sample H pulse. Each frame starts with a distinctive 168-sample V marker using reserved extreme levels. The V12 RF firmware keeps the same 120,000-samples/s PCM8 and MPX3 serial transport.
